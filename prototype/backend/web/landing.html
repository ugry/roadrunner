Good questions

Frequently asked

How fast will help actually arrive?

Our global average from confirmed request to arrival is under 45 minutes, and often far less in cities. You'll see a live, honest ETA the moment a provider is matched — no vague "sometime today" windows.

Do you cover other countries?

Yes. Insucar operates in more than 200 countries and territories through a network of 12,000+ verified providers. Plus and Global members are covered across borders automatically — no paperwork before you travel.

What if I drive an electric vehicle?

EVs are first-class citizens. We dispatch EV-trained crews with the right loading equipment, offer a mobile charge boost where available, or flatbed you to the nearest fast charger. No towing damage, no drama.

How do you find the nearest provider?

When you drop your pin, we match your exact situation to the closest available, verified crew and confirm in seconds. 97% of rescues are then geo-tracked end to end so you always know where your help is.

Can I add my family or a second car?

Absolutely. The Global plan covers up to five vehicles and everyone in your household, whoever is driving. You can add or swap vehicles anytime from your account.

Is there a contract or can I cancel?

No lock-in. Start on pay-as-you-go for free, upgrade whenever you like, and cancel a membership in one tap. You keep cover until the end of the period you've already paid for.
